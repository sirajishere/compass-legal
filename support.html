How does the free trial work?

New accounts can try every feature free for 14 days, with no payment. When the trial ends, Compass Focus is locked until you choose a plan: Monthly, Yearly, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Your data stays safe while it's locked. One free trial is available per account and per device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Compass Focus → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the app: You → Your Plan → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: You → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all its data.
