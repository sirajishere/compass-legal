Privacy Policy

Last updated · September 2026

Compass is a personal productivity app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it. We keep it short because we collect little.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, when you create an account.
  • Your content: the tasks, habits, goals, study subjects, projects, streaks, moods and settings you create in the app.
  • Community activity: comments you post to the community feed and messages you send us through in-app support.
  • Diagnostics: anonymous crash reports and basic in-app usage counts (for example, how many items you complete) that help us fix bugs and improve the app.
  • A random install identifier: created by the app and kept in your device’s secure storage, used only to allow one free trial per device. It is linked to your account while the account exists. A record that this identifier has used its free trial, with no personal information, is kept after account deletion solely to prevent free-trial abuse.

Where your data lives

Your content is stored on your device first, so the app works offline. While you are signed in, it is also backed up to your private account on Google Firebase so you can restore it and sync between devices. We use Firebase for sign-in, database backup, crash reporting and notifications; Google processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

To run the app, back up and sync your data, respond to your feedback, fix crashes, improve features and prevent free-trial abuse. That is all.

What we do NOT do

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not show third-party ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not access your location, contacts or photos.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

Your choices & deleting your data

You can edit or delete any individual item at any time. You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated cloud data from Profile → Delete account. Signing out or uninstalling removes the app's data from your device.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and access to your cloud data is restricted to your own account by server-side security rules.

Children

Compass is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will change the date shown above.
