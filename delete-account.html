Delete your account

Compass Focus by Hamza Awan

You can delete your Compass Focus account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  • Open Compass Focus and go to the You tab.
  • Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.
  • Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the app, email mhamzaawan06@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and sign-in.
  • Everything you created: tasks, habits, goals, projects, study, progress and settings saved to your account.
  • Your comments in the community feed, your messages with us, and your accountability-duo membership.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A record that your device has used its free trial. It holds only a device identifier, no personal information, and is kept to prevent repeated free trials.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google Play and our payment provider (RevenueCat), as required for billing, tax and refunds.
  • Analytics and crash reports already collected, no longer linked to your account, kept for up to 14 months and then deleted.

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
